# Bounded volition and lived consequences

September 9, 2026. This is an implementation and evaluation record, not a claim
that Patrick is conscious or possesses metaphysical free will.

## What changed

Patrick's planning model no longer receives a flat omniscient menu and picks the
highest score. A deterministic attention layer first admits three or four felt
possibilities from current momentum, bodily needs, accepted plans, domestic load,
noticed opportunities and private thought. Each has pull and friction, but neither
is action authority. Unattended alternatives are absent from that decision.

Motivation and scheduling are now separate model calls. The private deliberative
layer sees the attended possibilities and may choose one, continue, wait, defer or
do nothing. A quiet choice stops there and does not call a scheduling model. If an
impulse is chosen, its stated intention becomes a durable event before the planner
runs. The planner receives that chosen intention and practical context, but not the
competing needs and opportunities that might tempt it to silently choose again.
Alignment validation rejects a substitute plan—for example, choosing hunger and
then scheduling dishes. Feasibility still belongs to deterministic world rules:
opening hours, travel, calendar, resources, known people and location can reject an
intention without rewriting it as success.

Accepted work now records Patrick's estimate and confidence separately from a
replay-stable hidden effort requirement. Confidence is subjective rather than a
truth switch: even confidence `1.0` permits a bounded eight-percent error, while
lower confidence widens the possible error to forty percent. Work can finish early
or run out of its reserved window. Conversations, phone calls, sleep, absence and
incidents stop the effort clock. Returning incurs interruption-specific switching
cost; a small optional session can be consciously shortened, but shortening never
means the original task was completed.

Attention is projected as open, occupied or engrossed. Engrossment can cause a
natural incoming call to be missed without pretending Patrick declined it. The
notification is noticed later and can create a callback plan.

Meal intentions now cross the physical boundary. A chosen meal reserves time but
cannot complete unless Patrick is awake and present, hunger still makes eating
meaningful, household provisions or cafe funds exist, and a real `meal.eaten` event
reduces hunger. Home food stock is consumed. Reflex meal behavior yields to a chosen
near-term meal instead of eating twice. A failed meal produces no accomplishment.
An irrelevant food-stock resource attached to dishwashing is retained as audited
input but normalized out of the executable plan.

The Life UI now exposes current absorption, the possibilities that reached
attention, the resulting choice, interruption-aware progress and uncertain time
estimates. It remains inspection only; these panels do not let the user choose for
Patrick.

## Real-model evidence

`infra/volition_trial.py` constructs synthetic histories only and never opens the
live database. On Qwen 2.5 14B, twelve contrasting decisions in
`data/evaluations/volition-qwen14b-20260909-v3/results.json` had a median response
time of 11.97 seconds. The model formed six plans, explicitly continued twice,
deferred once and made three infeasible attempts. In the two high-hunger cases it
once continued and once chose the standardized physical meal. Under exhaustion it
once chose a very small chore and once deferred. This is useful behavioral variation,
not proof of human cognition.

That trial also exposed systematic misuse of household food as dishwashing equipment.
The next one-repeat run in `volition-qwen14b-20260909-v5` shows the deterministic
normalization working: the accepted dish plans have `resource_id: null`, while the
raw requested resource remains auditable on the proposal. It also preserved a true
`continue` choice under exhaustion and rejected meal plans that could not fit before
travel to work.

The first trial still over-attended the visible dish burden, gave ordinary work
unrealistically perfect duration confidence, and often ignored the map opportunity
in the curious scenario. That exposed a structural confound: one prompt was both
selecting a motive and formatting its schedule. The two-stage design above removes
the scheduling examples from private deliberation and makes even perfect confidence
fallible. A fresh real-model comparison is still required before claiming that the
behavioral bias is corrected.

## Safety and epistemic boundary

- Randomness breaks ties and supplies uncertainty; it does not grant permission.
- A model proposal is desire or deliberation, never a completed event.
- A planning model cannot replace the impulse selected in private deliberation.
- Exact hidden effort is available to operator inspection but not Patrick's personal
  context.
- Old memories remain fallible; current execution evidence does not repair them
  globally.
- Quiet time, indecision, failed intentions and unfinished work are valid outcomes.
- The live server world was left paused and unchanged during this work.
